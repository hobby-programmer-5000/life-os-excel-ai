The 35 daily protocols in this project are not generic self-help slogans—every single habit is grounded in primary works and empirical mechanisms across **behavioral psychology, neuroscience, longevity medicine, management science, value investing, and Stoic philosophy**, structured across **7 Life Domains (L1–L7)**.

## 🧭 Curation & 7-Domain Architecture

Sustainable high performance cannot come from work productivity alone. Cognitive execution (**L1**) depends on circadian & physiological recovery (**L2**), emotional regulation (**L3**), compounding intellectual depth (**L4**), disciplined collaboration (**L5**), financial independence (**L6**), and low-friction behavioral systems (**L7**).
