## 1. Lucide Icons (`assets/icons/lucide/`)

* **Upstream Project:** [Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev) ([github.com/lucide-icons/lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide))
* **License:** ISC License (with upstream Feather Icons subset under the MIT License)
* **Local License Copy:** [`assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE`](assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE)
* **How Used:**
  * Loaded dynamically as crisp anti-aliased Tkinter icons via [`ui_theme.py`](ui_theme.py) across the main floating assistant, Settings dialog, Mobile Bot & Webhook dialog, Full 35 Checklist window, and Keyword Guide.
  * Used directly (specifically the **`trending-up`** Growth Graph motif) to generate the unified application icons [`assets/app_icon.ico`](assets/app_icon.ico) and [`assets/app_icon.png`](assets/app_icon.png) via [`app_icon.py`](app_icon.py) for the Windows executable, system tray, and child dialog title bars.
